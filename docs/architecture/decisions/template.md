# NNNN. Use a present-tense imperative decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Recorded: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision state in code: Planned | Partially implemented | Implemented
- Supersedes: None
- Superseded by: None

## Context

Describe the architecturally significant problem, constraints, quality attributes,
and evidence available when the decision is proposed. Separate facts from
assumptions and unresolved questions.

## Decision

State one decision in direct, testable language.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

Explain why it was not selected, including relevant benefits and costs.

### Alternative B

Explain why it was not selected, including relevant benefits and costs.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Describe outcomes that follow from the decision.

### Negative and trade-offs

- Describe costs, restrictions, operational burden, and failure modes.

## Evidence

- Link implementation paths, tests, protocol captures, external standards, or
  project documentation.
- Record the boundary of what the evidence proves.

## Fitness functions

- List automated checks or manual validation that demonstrate the decision remains
  implemented.

## Review triggers

- List concrete changes that require this ADR to be revisited or superseded.

## Related decisions

- Link related ADRs and explain the relationship.

## Notes

Add later information as a dated note. Do not silently rewrite the original
context or decision.
